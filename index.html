About PT. Media Mandiri

PT. Media Mandiri is a trusted media representative in Indonesia, established in January 1997. We serve as the official representative for select global media outlets, bridging Indonesian companies that aim to expand their reach and build awareness in international markets. Supported by a team of seasoned professionals with extensive media industry experience, we are serving both local and global clients.
